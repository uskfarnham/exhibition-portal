# Farnham Urban Sketchers & Friends

## Exhibition Portal — Stewarding Guide

A guide for volunteers helping with hand-in, recording sales during the show, and takedown. No login is needed — just your name and a shared PIN, given to you by the exhibition organiser.

---
<br/>

### 1. Getting set up
---
The first time you use any scan-update page or the Item/Card Status screens on a device, you'll be asked to select your name from a list and enter the shared PIN. This is remembered on that device afterwards, so you won't need to re-enter it each time you use it during your shift.

If your name isn't in the list, let the organiser know — they can add you.

<br/>

### 2. Hand-in day
---
As artists arrive with their work:
- Check each item against the printed **Master List** (or **Card Master List** for card batches) as it's handed in.
- Mark each item as **Received**, either:
  - On the **Item Status Table** / **Card Sales Status** screen directly, or
  - By scanning the QR code on the item's own label, which opens a single-item **Update Item Status** (or **Update Card Sale**) page — quicker if you're moving between items rather than sat at a screen.

<br/>

### 3. During the exhibition — recording sales
---
When something sells:
- Find the item on the **Item Status Table**, or scan its label to open its single-item update page.
- Set its status (e.g. Sold – Awaiting Collection), and record the sale price, payment method/reference, and the buyer's signature.
- For card sales, use the **Card Sales Status** screen (or scan the card batch's QR) and tap **+** for each card sold — it updates the running tally instantly.

**Concurrent updates are protected.** If two people try to update the same item at the same time, the second save will show a conflict message rather than silently overwriting the first — just follow the on-screen prompt to reload and try again.

<br/>

### 4. Closing and takedown
---
- Mark any unsold items as **Returned** once collected by the artist or taken down.
- For card batches, mark them **Received** back (if not already) and record the physically-counted number of unsold cards using **Record Return Count** — this compares automatically against what the system expects and flags any mismatch for the organiser to follow up.
- The organiser may ask for help doing a final physical count against the **Card Closeout Sheet**.

<br/>

---
<br/>

### 5. Fallback: if the system is unreachable
---
If you lose signal or can't get online during hand-in or the show, don't hold up the queue — fall back to paper and catch up later:

- **Items:** use the printed **Master List**. It has columns for Received / Sold / Sale Price / Signature / Returned — fill these in by hand exactly as you would on-screen.
- **Cards:** use the printed **Card Master List**. It has a Received checkbox and ruled tally lines per batch — tally sales by hand as they happen.
- **Catching up:** as soon as you're back online (or hand your paper sheet to someone who is), enter everything from the paper sheet into the **Item Status Table** / **Card Sales Status** screen so the digital record matches. Do this as soon as practical — the Sold List and Closing Summary reports are generated from the digital records, not the paper sheets.
- If you're not sure whether something's already been entered (e.g. a shift handover), check the **Item Audit Log** / **Card Sales Audit Log** with the organiser rather than guessing — it shows exactly what's been recorded and when.

<br/>

---

<br/>

## Quick reference: what to use when

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| **Checking items in at hand-in** | Master List (paper) + Item Status Table, or scan each label |
| **Checking card batches in** | Card Master List (paper) + Card Sales Status, or scan each batch's QR |
| **Recording a sale** | Item Status Table, or scan the item's QR |
| **Recording a card sale** | Card Sales Status +/- tap, or scan the card batch's QR |
| **No signal / system unreachable** | Fill in the paper Master List / Card Master List, then enter it into the system once back online |
| **Marking items/cards returned at close** | Item Status Table / Card Sales Status |
| **Physical card recount at close** | Card Closeout Sheet (paper) + Record Return Count on-screen |
| **Something looks wrong** | Ask the organiser to check the Item Audit Log / Card Sales Audit Log |
