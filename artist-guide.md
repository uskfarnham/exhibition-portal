# Farnham Urban Sketchers & Friends

## Exhibition Portal — Artist Guide

A walk-through of the whole process from your invitation to collecting any unsold work, covering what you'll do on the portal and what to expect at each stage. For the full rules and conditions of entry, see the separate Rules document — this guide focuses on using the system itself.

---
<br/>

### 1. Your invitation
---
You'll receive an email invitation with a link to the exhibition portal's gateway page. From here you'll find all the exhibition's documentation and tools — click **"Start an Entry"** to open your own blank entry form. No account or login needed.

<br/>

### 2. Submitting your entry
---
- Fill in your personal details, artist statement, and profile photo.
- Add your items across the relevant categories (Hanging, Sculpture, Folios, Sketchbooks, Cards) — up to the limits shown on the form for each.
- Optionally tick **"Show my contact details"** and add a website or social media handle. If ticked, these appear on your public Artist Info page (see Section 7).
- Use **Preview Before Saving** to check how your profile and items will look.
- Tick the terms box and submit.
- A confirmation email arrives with your own personal link — save this. It's how you return to edit your entry at any point before submissions close, and it stays valid through to the end of the process.

**Important — complete all the boxes for each entry.**  Incomplete items will not be included in the exhibition lists and labels once submissions are closed.

<br/>

### 3. Editing your entry
---
Use your personal link any time before submissions close to add, edit, or remove items, update your statement or photo, or change your contact-details preference.

<br/>

### 4. Submissions closing
---
Once the exhibition organiser closes submissions, your entry becomes **read-only** automatically. You'll no longer be able to edit — but you can still:
- Preview and print your profile.
- Generate your artwork labels (see below).

<br/>

### 5. Printing your labels
---
Once submissions are closed **and** item numbering has been finalised, use **"Generate My Artwork Labels"** on your (now read-only) portal page. This produces a PDF with three copies of each label, showing your item's title, medium, price, permanent item number, and a QR code.

**Important — print at 100% scale.** Using "fit to page" in your printer dialog can cut into the QR code and stop it scanning, even though the label still looks fine.

<br/>

### 6. Handing in your work
---
Bring your artwork to hand-in, with labels attached as described in the Rules document. Your work will be checked in against the exhibition's master checklist.

<br/>

### 7. Your public Artist Info page
---
If the exhibition admin enabled it, a QR code on your wall label links visitors to your public Artist Info page — showing your photo, statement, and everything else you have on show (with the option to browse your other pieces too). If you opted in, your contact details appear here as well.

<br/>

### 8. If your work sells
---
Sales are recorded by exhibition volunteers directly against your item's record as they happen (status, sale price, and who bought it). You don't need to do anything on the portal for this — it's tracked automatically behind the scenes.

A processing fee and the exhibition's commission are deducted from the sale price before the remaining amount is owed to you (see the Rules document for current rates). Payment of amounts owed is arranged with the exhibition organiser after the show closes and figures are reconciled.

If you sell a piece privately after submitting your entry but before the exhibition, see the Rules document for your options (exhibiting it anyway and paying commission, or withdrawing/replacing it by agreement with the organiser).

<br/>

### 9. Collecting unsold work
---
At the end of the exhibition, any unsold work must be collected and signed out at the desk during the advertised collection times (see the Rules document for exact times). If someone is collecting on your behalf, let the organiser know in advance.

<br/>

## Quick reference: your key links

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Submit / edit your entry** | Your personal link, from your confirmation email |
| **Print your artwork labels** | Same personal link, once submissions close and numbering is finalised |
| **Your public Artist Info page** | Linked via the QR code on your own wall label — you don't need to visit this yourself |
| **Rules and conditions of entry** | Separate Rules document — commission rates, framing, hand-in/collection times, and full terms |
